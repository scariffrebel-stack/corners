How to play

  1. Agree the stake. Everyone puts in the same, like £5 each or losers buy a round. Settle up between yourselves: Corners never touches money.
  2. Someone gets the flag. At kick-off the order is shuffled and the first player holds the Corner flag.
  3. Every corner passes it on. Each corner to either team moves the flag one place along. The app follows the live match for you.
  4. Hold it at full time, take the pot. Whoever has the flag when the ref blows wins.

Extra: the golden corner. A secret corner from 1 to 12 wins whoever gets the flag on it a prize you choose.

Play a few times with the same mates and a table of who has won most shows up.
