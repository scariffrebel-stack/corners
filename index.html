How to play

  1. Put in some tokens. Everyone sets their own stake for the match on their phone. Leave it blank to sit one out.
  2. Someone gets the flag. At kick-off the order is shuffled and the first player holds the Corner flag.
  3. Every corner passes it on. Each corner to either team moves the flag one place along. The app follows the live match for you.
  4. Hold it at full time, win the pot. Whoever has the flag when the ref blows takes every token staked.

Extras: guess the total corners for a side pot, or tick golden corner, where a secret corner wins a quarter of the pot.

Play a single match, a tournament or a whole season. Each keeps its own tokens and its own table. Tokens are just for fun between friends, and the app never takes or pays out money.
